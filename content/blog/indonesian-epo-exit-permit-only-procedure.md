## Indonesian Exit Permit Only (EPO) Procedure

An Indonesian Exit Permit Only (EPO) is a visa permit issued by Indonesian immigration authorities to foreigners intending to leave Indonesia without plans to return on their current visa. It cancels the existing visa's validity and permits exit without re-entry rights.

### Key Features

**Purpose**: The EPO officially terminates a foreigner's stay in Indonesia under their current visa status. This commonly applies when employment, studies, or other sponsored activities end.

**Application Process**: Applicants submit documents through their local immigration office, including a passport, current visa, and sponsorship letter from their employer or institution.

**Processing Timeline**: Generally takes several days to one week. Early application before departure is recommended.

**Visa Cancellation**: Once issued, the current visa becomes invalid. Departing foreigners must leave within the specified timeframe on the EPO document.

**No Re-entry Allowed**: The permit does not permit returning to Indonesia on the same visa. Future visits require applying for a new visa.

### Common Scenarios

- Completion or termination of employment for work visa holders
- Completion of studies for student visa holders
- End of stay for family or dependent visa holders

### Required Documentation

- Original passport with current visa
- EPO application form
- Sponsor letter (from employer or educational institution)
- Passport photocopy and visa stamp copy
- Flight ticket
- Sponsor identification
- Company legality documentation (for employer sponsors)
- Company NIB (registration number)

### Steps to Obtain an EPO

1. Gather all necessary documents
2. Visit the nearest immigration office
3. Submit application form with required documents
4. Await processing while immigration authorities verify details
5. Receive the approved EPO
6. Depart Indonesia within the specified timeframe
