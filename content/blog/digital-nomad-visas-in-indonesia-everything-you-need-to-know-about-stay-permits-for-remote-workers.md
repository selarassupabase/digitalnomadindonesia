## What is a Digital Nomad Visa?

A **Digital Nomad Visa** is designed for remote workers employed by foreign companies or operating their own online businesses. Unlike standard work visas, it permits individuals to work remotely from Indonesia without requiring employment from a local employer. Indonesia is currently developing this visa framework to streamline legal protections for remote workers, expected to offer more flexible terms than existing visa categories.

## Visa Options for Digital Nomads in Indonesia

### Tourist Visa (Visa on Arrival – VOA)

The **Visa on Arrival** permits stays up to **30 days**, extendable for an additional 30 days. However, working (remotely or otherwise) while on a VOA is **not permitted**, making it unsuitable for remote workers seeking legal employment authorization.

### B211A Social, Cultural, or Business Visa

The **B211A Visa** allows stays of up to **60 days** with potential extensions totaling **6 months**. This visa suits remote workers employed by foreign companies, though it prohibits income generation from Indonesian employers. It's particularly popular among digital nomads due to its flexibility and extended timeframes.

### Digital Nomad Visa

Indonesia's emerging **Digital Nomad Visa** will facilitate stays ranging from **6 months to 2 years** with extension possibilities. This visa enables legal remote work for foreign employers without restrictions common to other categories, potentially including benefits like co-working space access and affordable living support.

### KITAS (Temporary Stay Permit)

The **KITAS** (Kartu Izin Tinggal Terbatas) permits stays up to **2 years** with extension options. While traditionally designed for those working with Indonesian companies, digital nomads can obtain KITAS with an Indonesian citizen or local company sponsor. This option suits long-term residents but involves more stringent requirements than B211A visas.

## How to Apply for a Digital Nomad Visa in Indonesia

**Step 1: Prepare Required Documents**

- Valid passport with at least 6 months validity
- Proof of financial stability (bank statements, income sources)
- Health insurance covering your stay duration
- Return flight ticket or onward travel proof
- Additional documents depending on visa type

**Step 2: Submit the Application**

Apply through your nearest Indonesian consulate, embassy, or Indonesia's immigration portal. B211A applications require completed forms, supporting business or social activity documentation, and visa fees.

**Step 3: Wait for Approval**

Processing typically requires around **7-14 business days** for approval, though timelines vary by location.

**Step 4: Visa Extension**

B211A visas extend up to 6 months; KITAS extends to a maximum of 2 years.

## Living and Working in Indonesia as a Digital Nomad

Indonesia attracts remote workers through its **low cost of living**, **excellent internet infrastructure**, and **vibrant expat community**. Popular hubs like Bali, Jakarta, and Yogyakarta host numerous co-working spaces and networking events supporting digital nomad productivity and collaboration.
